Fractional CFO vs Finance Director: What Growing UK Businesses Actually Need

Most UK businesses between £1M and £30M in revenue face the same question: do we need a finance director, a CFO, or both? The answer depends on where you are in your growth journey and what financial challenges you are facing.

The Finance Director Handles Operations

A finance director manages the day-to-day finance function. Month-end close, statutory accounts, compliance, payroll oversight, VAT returns, and team management. They keep the financial engine running smoothly. This is essential work, but it is fundamentally operational.

The CFO Drives Strategy

A CFO operates at a different level. They build the three-year financial plan, lead fundraising conversations, design the capital allocation framework, and present to the board with strategic insight rather than just reporting numbers. The CFO connects financial data to business decisions.

Why the Fractional Model Works

A full-time CFO costs £150,000 to £250,000 in salary alone. Most businesses in the £2M to £15M range cannot justify that cost, nor can they attract the calibre of CFO they need at that stage. A fractional CFO provides CFO-level strategic thinking on a flexible basis — typically one to three days per week — at 30 to 50 percent of the full-time cost.

The fractional CFO works alongside your existing finance director or manager, adding the strategic layer that is missing. They attend board meetings, lead the fundraising process, build the financial model, and manage investor relationships.
